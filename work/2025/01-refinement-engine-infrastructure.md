## Summary

I contributed to the Refinement Engine (RE) development across multiple work streams including feature building, search metadata configuration, and evaluation through the development iteration.

## Context & Problem

The Refinement Engine is Search Foundational Technologies' system for storing and querying navigation data from multiple browse sources. In 2024, RE launched for refinement selection in US, replacing the existing ARPS system. Continued development required feature building, pre-weblab evaluation, and expansion to additional marketplaces (DE, UK).

## What I Did

I was involved in multiple work streams during RE development: feature building, feature configuration at search metadata level, and evaluation through the development iteration cycle. I conducted pre-weblab evaluations for multiple weblabs (SEARCH_SPT_1043600, SEARCH_SES_NAV_1190849 DE/UK, SEARCH_SPT_1200343 No-PT US).

## Impact & Results

Supported successful RE rollout and expansion across multiple marketplaces with validated evaluation results for each weblab.
