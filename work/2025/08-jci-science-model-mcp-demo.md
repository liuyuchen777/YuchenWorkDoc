## Summary

I built the JCI Science Model MCP as a hackathon project during the JCI + JST hackathon. The demo received strong positive reception from VP Thomas Grant (VP Japan Consumer Innovation), leading to an invitation to present at JCI All Hands. The project was subsequently adopted into JCI TEDS 2026 OP1 planning as foundation infrastructure, which I scaled into JP Retail MCP in 2026.

## Context & Problem

Vendor managers needed self-service access to 1P retail business data and science model outputs, but existing tools required manual spreadsheet analysis or waiting for BIE support. The hackathon was an opportunity to demonstrate that AI-powered natural language interaction with business data (via MCP) could solve this gap.

## What I Did

I built an end-to-end MCP demo during the hackathon that showed vendor managers querying retail metrics, generating business insights, and running analysis through natural language. The demo impressed VP Thomas Grant enough to earn an invitation to present at JCI All Hands, giving the project org-wide visibility.

After the hackathon, the project was included in JCI TEDS 2026 OP1 as a foundation infrastructure item. I continued development in 2026, scaling the prototype into JP Retail MCP — a production platform serving multiple teams.

## Impact & Results

- VP-level endorsement from Thomas Grant (L10, VP Japan Consumer Innovation)
- Invited to present at JCI All Hands
- Adopted into JCI TEDS 2026 OP1 as foundation infrastructure
- Evolved into JP Retail MCP serving 115+ metrics across vendor business, ASIN content quality, and promotion analysis
