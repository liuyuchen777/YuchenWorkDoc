## Summary

I was involved in the early engineering design discussions for the PromoPro Analytics (Paid Service) module, working cross-functionally with JPoP, PST, and ProServe teams to define how science and data teams collaborate. I hosted a mini-workshop between JPoP SDEs and PromoPro Scientists to build early consensus on the science solution approach.

## Context & Problem

PromoPro is a paid service product that provides vendors with promotion analytics — including OPS uplift benchmarking and promotion efficiency scoring. The engineering design phase required alignment across multiple teams (JPoP for product engineering, PST, ProServe) on how to integrate science model outputs into the product. A key risk was misalignment between what the science models produce and what engineering can feasibly render in the UI, leading to costly rework downstream.

## What I Did

I participated in the early engineering design of the PromoPro Analytics module, working with JPoP, PST, and ProServe teams to develop a plan for science/data team collaboration.

I organized and hosted a "Tech2Tech" mini-workshop (December 5, 2025) bringing together JPoP engineering (Koji Endo, Changyuan Xie, Kosate Limpongsa, and others) and PromoPro Science (Pawel Matuszyk, Yuhao Wang) to establish shared understanding before feature development. The workshop covered OPS Uplift Benchmarking, Promotion Efficiency Scoring, and a working-backwards session bridging UI designs (Figma) to science data schemas. The goals were to align on data semantics, refresh cadence, edge case handling (missing data, negative uplift), and establish escalation paths for data quality issues.

## Impact & Results

The workshop established shared vocabulary and communication patterns between engineering and science teams, reducing ambiguity in data interpretation and enabling the JPoP team to begin benchmarking view development with validated assumptions. This early alignment prevented costly rework by surfacing constraints and edge cases before feature implementation began.
